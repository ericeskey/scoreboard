# CRUX: The one challenge that untangles the rest (client-ready source for Claude Design)

PAGE INTENT: Candidate cruxes as comparable surfaces. Matt weighs them against each other and picks (or amends) the one that rings true. The chosen crux becomes the commit page's object.

PAGE HEADLINE: The crux. Five candidates, weighed against each other.
PAGE SUBTEXT: A crux is the single challenge that, if untangled, untangles everything downstream. Here are five candidates from the record, each critiqued honestly. One of them carries more leverage than the rest.

---

## CARD: THE DIAGNOSIS (lead panel, not a candidate)
BODY: The direction is ratified. The pieces are specified. The risk is the graveyard: Teleflex sustaining has repeatedly produced finished designs that arrived before their evidence.
EXPAND: Three prior attempts followed the same arc. A 10-KPI draft dashboard was rejected as too much. A financial-KPI proposal was presented two years ago and never landed. An IPN-connection project got so complex it was put on hold. In each case: right idea, no evidence base underneath it, no decision the audience had to make about it. The pivotal challenge is therefore not design and not approval; both exist in draft. It is credibility of the first instance. The first quarterly EPR must survive a skeptical room using only data that actually reconciles, and must end in at least one decision only that room could make. If the first instance is credible, everything compounds. If it is another almost, it joins the graveyard with better formatting.

## CARD X1: Finish the design now
BODY: Lock the agenda, cut the KPIs to 3 to 5, ship the format.
CRITIQUE: Low untangling power. The cut cannot be made honestly yet: each measure's evidence strength depends on data still arriving (the INTV dashboard, the SKU files, the source-of-truth outcome). Cutting on judgment now reruns the exact failure already lived twice. The finished design is the output of the evidence work, not its precondition.

## CARD X2: Run the evidence pass
BODY: Test all twelve candidate KPIs against the data that actually exists. Let the surviving 3 to 5 carry their own defense. Build the first quarterly on only what reconciles.
CRITIQUE: Highest untangling power. This one move produces the defensible KPI cut, surfaces exactly where the truth fracture bites (turning an unowned governance question into named roadmap items leadership can arbitrate), determines what the first quarterly can honestly show, and answers Andrej empirically rather than rhetorically. It is also the move no prior scoreboard attempt ever ran: every previous effort went straight from idea to artifact. Its honest weakness: it needs data conduits and cooperation across BUs, which is exactly why it is the right first joint piece of work rather than a solo task.

## CARD X3: Force the source-of-truth decision first
BODY: Resolve which record governs project status before designing anything on top.
CRITIQUE: Real but not primary. The fracture gates the financial column and some near-term measures, but attacking it head-on repeats the stalemate (nobody wants to answer) at higher volume. The evidence pass subsumes it: the discovery shows which measures wait on the decision, and the quarterly's decisions agenda hands the arbitration to the only people who can make it. Expose it on the instrument; don't broker it from below.

## CARD X4: Design the room first
BODY: Settle who sits in the quarterly before settling what it shows.
CRITIQUE: Medium power, and largely resolved by the record already. The survey respondents are the natural candidates, each with a stated stake, and the final call is co-owned with Harsh on his return. Carrying the room as a visible open question inside the socialization is stronger than deciding it in a vacuum now: the respondents see themselves in the design.

## CARD X5: Paper first: land the SOW, then do the work inside it
BODY: The ask was made. Put the engagement on paper immediately.
CRITIQUE: Commercially attractive, sequentially wrong by days. The cleanest paper simply names the evidence pass as its scope, so the shape conversation belongs this week or next, while first steps on already-shared data can begin in parallel. Paper that precedes the ratified direction's socialization is weaker paper. Sequence: shape now, sign against the socialized direction, discover.

## CARD: THE CRUX, STATED ONCE (closing panel)
BODY: Run the evidence pass no prior scoreboard attempt ever ran. Test the twelve candidates against the data that exists. Let the surviving 3 to 5 carry their own defense. Build the first quarterly on only what reconciles, and end it in at least one decision only that room can make.
EXPAND: This single discipline converts the ratified direction into a credible first instance, answers Andrej's test empirically, turns the truth fracture into leadership's named decision, and gives Jake Newman's question its first honest answer. It is also, by its nature, a bounded piece of work with a clear start, a clear output, and a clear finish. That is what the next page envisions and what the commit page proposes.
