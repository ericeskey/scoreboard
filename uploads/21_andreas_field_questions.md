# 21 — Andreas Field-Question Sheet (kickoff day-1 backbone; DHW working paper)

**Purpose: terrain questions in frequency form. "Of the last ten..." gets calibrated answers where "is it reliable?" gets shrugs. Also relationship work: arriving with specific, answerable questions shows a precise builder that we respect precision. Test cheap claims against the snapshots in the room; record expert claims as probability statements, not facts.**

## Systems and structure

1. Walk us through what happens in the systems when a new sustaining project is created: which record exists first, and what references it?
2. Of the last ten projects entered in Planview, how many were created within a week of the work actually starting?
3. When a project slips, where does the new date land: overwritten, versioned, or noted somewhere else?
4. Which fields in the Planview extract are auto-populated versus hand-entered? Of the hand-entered ones, which three are most trustworthy, and which three least?
5. The INTV dashboard's revenue-continuity page computes dollars at risk per project. Where do those dollar figures come from, field by field?

## The financial path (SKU to IPN to SAP)

6. Of the last ten sustaining projects, how many had impacted SKUs identified anywhere in the record at intake? By close?
7. For a SKU in hand, what are the actual steps today to get from SKU to internal part number? Who can do it, and how long does one take?
8. Of ten SKU-to-IPN lookups, how many resolve cleanly versus needing judgment (superseded parts, multiples, regional variants)?
9. What does SAP hold per part that we would want: revenue, standard cost, margin, plant? What access exists and who has it?
10. If we hand-traced 20 projects end to end during the kickoff, who would need to be in the room, and for roughly how long?

## The other BUs

11. Vascular, Surgical, and Anesthesia/EM project lists: which have you seen, and of what you've seen, what fraction of INTV's field coverage do they have?
12. If asked to produce an INTV-style dashboard for another BU tomorrow, what is the FIRST thing that would block you?

## Load and history

13. Of the requests reaching the command center in a typical month, how many are report/dashboard requests? How many died for lack of final requirements?
14. What has been asked of you before that sounded like this project? What happened to it?
15. If you could fix one data problem in the sustaining portfolio and have it stay fixed, which one, and why that one?

## Discipline notes (for Eric)

- Questions 14 and 15 are the trust questions; leave the most time for them.
- Record answers as claims-with-confidence, not facts. Anything checkable against a snapshot in under five minutes, check live (question 8 especially).
- The one-and-done posture to hold throughout: we are here so that what he eventually builds, he builds once. Say it early; it is his own requirement, honored.
