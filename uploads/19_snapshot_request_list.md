## Ground rules

- One-time snapshots, not feeds. Nothing recurring, nothing built.
- As-is beats cleaned-up. Blank fields, stale dates, and inconsistencies are findings, not embarrassments; they are what the reachability map is made of.
- Handled under the confidentiality terms in place; returned or destroyed at engagement end, at Teleflex's direction.
- Anything that cannot be extracted is itself useful to know early: tell us what blocked it and that goes on the map too.
