# COMMIT: The first piece of work (client-ready source for Claude Design)

PAGE INTENT: The commitment draft. Bounded, affirmable, and explicitly a draft for reaction, not a contract. NO PRICING ANYWHERE (hard rule: price goes in the SOW, per-action approved). The page converts the affirmed ladder into a scoped first engagement and a concrete next step.

PAGE HEADLINE: The first piece of work, drafted to be argued with.
PAGE SUBTEXT: Everything above this page was free and stays free. This page proposes the first bounded engagement: the evidence pass. React to the scope; the paper follows the reaction.

---

## CARD CM1: The object
BODY: Phase 1: the evidence pass. Test the twelve candidate KPIs against Teleflex's real data. Deliver the defensible headline set, the reachability map, the first quarterly's design, and the socialization set in stakeholders' own words.
EXPAND: Four deliverables. One: the 3-to-5 headline KPI cut, each measure carrying its evidence (why it is on the dashboard, what data feeds it, what it can answer). Two: the reachability map for all twelve candidates: live-now versus build, with each build item's dependency named (IPN linkage, source-of-truth decision, SKU coverage). Three: the first quarterly EPR's run-of-show: the five beats populated with real content, the room recommendation, the decision docket for beat 4. Four: the socialization set: the artifacts that carry the design to Harsh, the survey respondents, and the EPR room in their own words, forwardable as-is.

## CARD CM2: The shape
BODY: Four to six weeks. Runs alongside the automation lane, never inside it. Built to relieve load: synthesis absorbed, artifacts forwardable, nothing to rewrite.
EXPAND: The work consumes what already exists or is already arriving: the two survey waves, the INTV Power BI dashboard, Surgical's SKU file, Planview extracts, and the prioritization framework. Weekly working rhythm: short check-ins on Matt's calendar terms, brain-dump channel stays open, drafts arrive in reviewable form. The automation lane (intake form, data collection, PBI, SharePoint) remains fully owned inside Teleflex; this work rides on its outputs and never blocks it.

## CARD CM3: What Phase 1 explicitly does not include
BODY: No dashboard build. No enterprise data-governance program. No new tooling. No financial-linkage construction. Those are Phase 2, scoped only after the evidence says what they should be.
EXPAND: Phase 1 stays small on purpose. The build work (extending dashboard views across BUs, proving the SKU-to-IPN-to-SAP beachhead, bringing build-column measures live) is named as Phase 2 and deliberately not scoped yet, because its right shape depends on what the evidence pass finds. Keeping Phase 1 bounded is what makes it fast to approve and honest to deliver.

## CARD CM4: What success looks like at the end of Phase 1
BODY: A first quarterly EPR that can be scheduled with confidence: an agenda with real content, headline measures that survive drilling, and a decision docket leadership recognizes as theirs.
EXPAND: The test is the graveyard test: prior scoreboard attempts died as finished designs without evidence. Phase 1 succeeds when the first quarterly's every element can answer "why is this here" with data, when Andrej's incremental-value question has an empirical answer, and when the socialization has already put the design in front of the room in their own words, so the first meeting confirms rather than introduces.

## CARD CM5: The immediate next step
BODY: A 30-minute shape conversation this week or early next, before Harsh returns. Direction and paper arrive together.
EXPAND: The conversation settles scope reactions from this page, the working rhythm, and the paper. The statement of work follows it directly. Timing matters: with the recommendations accepted and Harsh back around August 3, a shaped engagement waiting on his return is stronger than a conversation starting on it.

## CARD CM6: The standing record
BODY: Every reaction on this site joins the record: affirmations, corrections, additions. The design stays honest because it stays argued-with.
EXPAND: This site is the working surface for the engagement, in the same way the quarterly EPR will be the working surface for the portfolio. What gets affirmed here shapes the paper; what gets corrected here never reaches it. That is the method, demonstrated on itself.
